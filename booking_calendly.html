Booking Calendar – AllenPlace, 450 Century Parkway

To book the 450 Century Parkway, AllenPlace location’s Deli Area, Conference Room, or both:

  1. Click on the desired calendar date.
  2. Select the time slot(s) you want to reserve.
  3. Complete your contact details.
Please note: To book multiple hours for the Conference Room (for example, 6–8 PM), click on all related time blocks to reserve the full timeframe.
Looking for a month you don’t see? Select dates beyond the 3 months displayed by clicking on the upper-right triangle in the green calendar header. This will advance the calendar another month.
If the popup is blocked, allow popups for this site and try again.
